## Communication & Collaboration Method(s)

- **Communication**
    - **Discord** will be the primary method of communication.
    - **Email** will be used as a secondary method of communication if a member cannot be reached on discord.

- **Collaboration Tools**
    - **Google Drive (Docs)** will be used for document collaboration.
    - **Github** for version control and code collaboration.

- **Help & Deadlines**: 
    - Members are encouraged to **ask for help before soft deadlines** rather than miss them.

- **Escalation**: 
    - Program administration may be contacted, but members should first attempt to resolve issues internally.

## Client Communication
- **Any group member** may communicate with the client.
- **Written correspondence**
    - Proofread by **at least one other member** before sending. 
    - **CC all group members and instructors** on any emails.

## Punctuality & Attendance
- Meetings should be confirmed **at least one day in advance**.
- If unable to attend or arriving late: 
    - Notify the group **as soon as possible** (ideally the day before, minimum 30 minutes before the meeting if missing entirely).
- **Absentees are responsible** for catching up, reading meeting summaries, and completing assigned work.
- **Valid reasons**: illness, emergencies, non-reschedulable appointments, transit issues, work conflicts, or natural disasters.
- **Invalid reasons**: oversleeping, lack of motivation, waiting in line for coffee, etc.
- **Failure to notify/invalid absence**: Results in a strike (see Strikes).

## Meeting Times, Locations & Management
- **Default meetings**:
    - Capstone class time
    - Discord voice server project rooms
- **Additional meetings**: Schedule as required.
- **Decision making**: Requires a **minimum of 3 members (majority)**.
- **Task Assignment**: Only valid if the member is present or has confirmed agreement.
- **Post meetings**: Must include time, required members, and task descriptions (posted in Discord).
- **Cancellations**: Announced via Discord; email if a member doesn't confirm.

## Expectations
- All members must contribute a **reasonable share** of work.
- **Refusal to contribute** will result in a strike.
- **Deadlines**:
    - Each milestone will have **at least one soft deadline** (preferably two).
    - Members missing the **first soft deadline** must correct issues before the **second soft deadline**.
    - Failure to meet the second deadline --> **removal from the group** (see Removal Policy).
- Group work is subject to **all program policies and expectations**.

## Conflict Resolution
- Issues will first be discussed as a team.
- If unresolved --> **vote**.
- In case of a tie --> consult program administration.
- For minor disagreements:
    - If time allows, resolution may be decided via a **team chess match**.
    - Otherwise --> administration resolves.
- Severe infractions (policy violations, hate speech, etc). --> **immediate removal process**.

## Strikes
- A strike = a formal warning
- Strikes are logged in a **version-controlled document**.
- **Three strikes --> removal** (see Removal Policy).
- Illegal or policy-violating actions are escalated to **administration or law enforcement**.

## Member Removal Policy
- Evidence of infractions presented to the instructor.
- Upon instructor approval, member may be removed.
- Program administration will determine member's project status after removal.

## Coding Expectations
- **Front-end styling**: Maintain via **CSS/MUI**
- **Front-end to back-end communication**:
    - Front-end must provide data in the format required by the back-end (e.g., JSON, URL headers).
    - Front-end developers are responsible for formatting requests according to back-end requirements.
    - Back-end developers are responsible for formatting requests according to the front-end requirements.
